    Straight answers.

    Mechanism, limits, and blind spots.

    Why hold OGLY at all?+

    Backers wager on a cycle: gains become purchases, purchases become deletions. It succeeds only while the portfolio keeps profiting and buyers stay interested.

    What exactly gets burned?+

    Only coins acquired through the earmark. Coins sitting in other wallets stay untouched.

    Why are some numbers pending?+

    Numbers show up solely when a source backs them; otherwise the slot says pending, never an estimate.

    Which mint is genuine?+

    Whatever mint the top strip displays after publication. Treat every other string as unrelated.

    Is this advice?+

    No. Prices swing violently and value can vanish. Decide independently and stake only spare funds.
